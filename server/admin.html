The site ↗

Rounds

How many rounds the site has and which decoder made them - one an older decoder made is decoded again by itself after an update: from its demo, or from its page downloaded again. One whose demo is gone and that no page lists any more can't be: it stays as it was and opens as before (shown apart). Under it: the rounds of each game server, and the newest. Recalculate all records sums every round up again from its decoded file (no decoding, about 0.5 s a round, the site stays up): for when the Records page or the players' numbers look wrong.

Demos: what to keep

A round on the site lives in its decoded file (data/rounds): the map, the stats, the hints, the player pages. Its demo, 4-5 times bigger, is needed only to decode it again when the decoder changes and for a clip for the game. Old demos can go - their rounds stay and open as before.

Background analysis

What the buttons do
  • Run now: a run at once, instead of waiting 5 minutes.
  • Rounds at once: how many rounds it opens side by side, each in a browser page of its own (one core each). Automatic: the cores less two, at most 12 - the site and the decoding of new demos stay first (it runs at a low priority). A change takes effect within seconds, also while it runs; the table under it says how fast each number went here.
  • Pause: the rounds it is on are finished, then nothing until Go on (e.g. while the VPS is busy decoding).
  • Its log: its lines in the Log below.
  • Glitch spot: the maps' grids of where soldiers stood (a map needs enough rounds before it gives hints).
  • Highest suspicion scores: the players to look at first (their pages on the site, the Admin tab of their rounds).
  • Archive for Claude: every round's numbers in one file - make it, download it, send it to Claude to set the hints' thresholds, then remove it here.

Hints over all rounds (bf2replay-hints): every round opened in the site's page on the server, several at once, for the players' hint cards; and the grids of where soldiers stood, for Glitch spot. A run goes through every round that needs it - batches of 40 a page, the players' cards written after each, new rounds first - for up to 95 minutes; the next one starts 5 minutes after it ends. When it is near 100%, the archive below is what Claude needs to set the thresholds.

Hint thresholds

When each hint for admins fires: ours, recommended, set on thousands of real rounds - or the site's own. The site's thresholds are what the background analysis, the players' hint cards and the alerts use; changing them sends every round through the analysis again (it can take hours; the Background analysis card shows how far it got). Each admin can also set his own, for the rounds he opens. They are set in the site's page: the editor opens with the button below, from the Admin tab of a round, or from the Log in button.

Posts about new rounds

    
Each game server its own: its new rounds go only to its own Discord channel and / or Telegram chat, with the parts picked for it - the map, who won and the link (the chat shows the map under it) always. A server not set up here is not posted. Rounds downloaded in a batch (recorded more than 12 h before) and clips are not posted.

Servers

    
What each setting of a server does
  • Discord / Telegram: on or off, and where its round posts go - a channel's webhook, a chat. A server with neither is not posted.
  • Rounds with … players or more: smaller rounds are not posted and not counted in its summaries (empty: as for all, above).
  • Summaries: a post with the server's day, week or month - tick each to have it. Their own Discord channel (a webhook of their own; without one the round posts' channel - whether the round posts go there or not) and Telegram (the round posts' chat), each on or off. The summary: its title, a line under it, a text above the cards (where a mention pings: @here, a role), the bottom line, the stripe's colour, which parts, how many in the top, which leaders and bests, and their layout - exact columns, or bold numbers with a | before them (narrower on phones). Preview the summary shows the last period with the editor as it is now; Post it now sends it with the saved settings.
  • Admin channel: a private channel of the server's admins - after the background analysis of each new round, its hints for admins with the players' names; strong ones only, or strong and ordinary. Never the public channel.
  • The post: how a round post looks - classic, a card, a card with a table (a row each kind of kill) or text; which parts go to Discord and to Telegram, their names and order, an own text with {…}; templates to use on other servers, or Use on all servers.
  • Preview (its newest round, nothing sent), Send a test post, Post its newest round (for real).
  • Stop posting this server: its channels, post and summaries are forgotten; its rounds stay on the site.

    

    
Where to get these
Discord: the channel's settings (the gear) → Integrations → Webhooks → New Webhook → Copy Webhook URL. A channel a server: a webhook each.
Telegram: write to @BotFather, /newbot, copy the token it gives (one bot is enough for every server); add the bot to each group (or as an admin of each channel). The chat: @channelname for a public channel; for a group, its id - send a message in it, open https://api.telegram.org/bot<token>/getUpdates and take "chat":{"id": -100…}.
A server is known by the name in its demos: if its admins rename it, the new name shows here as a new server, not posted until set up.
Kept on the VPS only (data/.notify.json, which the web server never serves); here only the last 4 characters of the secrets show. In /etc/bf2replay.conf BF2R_TELEGRAM_TOKEN and BF2R_NOTIFY_MIN_PLAYERS win over these.

  


  

    
Users and access

    
Who may open what. Guests are visitors who are not logged in: untick a part of the site and it needs a login - untick all and the whole site is closed (a page then goes to the login page, /login). Accounts: each one may open what guests may and what is ticked for it - parts of the site, the Admin tab of its servers' rounds (hints and review), and cards of this page (Posts: only its servers'). An account's admin powers (the Admin tab, this page) last 12 h from its login; "keep me logged in" keeps only the watching for 30 days. You log in everywhere with an empty name and this page's password.

How to use this page

    
Every day
Nothing needs doing: demos are downloaded, decoded, posted and analysed by themselves. A glance is enough - Rounds grows, Being decoded empties, Disk has room, Services shows no "failed".

    
A new game server
  • Where demos come from: its demo page or its address → Check → Add. Its rounds show up under Rounds within minutes.
  • Posts about new rounds → open the server → its Discord webhook and / or Telegram chat → The post → Preview → Send a test post.
  • The Summaries under it: tick Day, Week or Month, choose their channel and look at The summary → Preview the summary.
  • Its admins: an Admin channel for the hints of each round, and accounts under Servers' admins for the Admin tab of the rounds on the site.

    
Who may open the site
  • Open to everyone: Users and access → Guests all ticked. Closed: untick the parts (or all) → Save; then each person an account: Add (a name, a password), open his row, tick what he may open (each tick is saved at once). A part ticked under Guests is open to every account too: its tick in an account changes nothing.
  • A server's admins: an account with the Admin tab and their servers ticked; for their posts and summaries too, the card Posts about new rounds (only their servers).
  • Someone can't get in: his row - a new password (Set), his rights. The login page is /login; a page closed to a visitor goes there by itself.

    
After an update
"Update from GitHub" looks for a new version every minute. When the decoder or the hint rules change, the old rounds are decoded and analysed again by themselves - watch Being decoded and Background analysis (the site works all the while).

    
When something is wrong
  • A service failed: the Log, that service - then its button to run it again.
  • Failed demos under Being decoded: "Try them again" (after an update most decode).
  • The disk nearly full: Demos: what to keep - fewer days or a GB limit; "What would go?" before Save.
  • A post or summary did not arrive: the server's "last post" / "last summaries" lines say why; then Send a test post.
  • A page of the site looks wrong after a change to the records: Recalculate all records.

    
Safe keeping
Webhooks, the bot's token and the passwords stay on the VPS (here only their last 4 characters show). Log out on a shared computer. This page's password is set on the VPS: sudo node /srv/bf2replay/repo/server/admin.mjs --set-password.
